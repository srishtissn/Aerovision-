For a high-severity crack indication, the aircraft should be removed from service until a qualified
inspector has confirmed and dispositioned the finding. Perform detailed non-destructive inspection
within 24 hours. Mark the crack tips, photograph the indication with a scale reference, and record
location and orientation. Do not return the aircraft to service until the structural repair manual
limits are met or an approved repair is complete. Stop-drilling is a temporary measure only where
the repair manual allows it.
