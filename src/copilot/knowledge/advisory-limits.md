Automated findings from image analysis and machine learning support a maintenance decision but do
not make it. Release to service is decided by appropriately certified maintenance personnel using
the approved maintenance data for the aircraft. All automated findings should be verified by a
qualified inspector, and any recommendation here must be traced to the inspection record or to the
cited guidance.
