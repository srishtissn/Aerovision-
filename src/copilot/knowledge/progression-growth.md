severity: "ANY"
source: "AeroVision starter KB: paraphrased general maintenance guidance (themes from FAA AC 43.13-1B Ch.4 Metal Structure and Ch.6 Corrosion; always defer to the aircraft SRM/AMM)"
action: "Treat any confirmed growth between scans as an escalation trigger and shorten the inspection interval"
interval: ""
---
A defect that is measurably larger than in an earlier scan is progressing and should be escalated
one level, whatever its current size. A new defect that was absent in the earlier scan should be
treated as a fresh finding and inspected promptly. A defect that appears smaller or resolved should
not be closed from imagery alone; confirm by a close inspection because lighting, angle or cleaning
can change how a defect looks. Comparisons are only reliable if the scans cover the same panel from
a similar angle.
