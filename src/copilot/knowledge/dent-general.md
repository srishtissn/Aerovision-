A dent (ding, impact damage or buckling of the skin) is a local depression with no loss of material. Whether it is acceptable depends on its
depth, its diameter, its sharpness, and its distance from fasteners, stiffeners and skin edges, all
of which are limited by the structural repair manual. Sharp-bottomed dents and dents with a crease
or paint cracking can hide cracks, so inspect the dent floor closely. A smooth, shallow dent away
from structural features is often acceptable to monitor.
