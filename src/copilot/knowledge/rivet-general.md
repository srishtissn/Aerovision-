Rivet damage includes missing or sheared heads, loose or working rivets, rivets with dark residue
streaks sometimes called smoking rivets, and corrosion around the head. A loose rivet lets the joint
move and can crack the surrounding skin. Inspect the neighbouring rivets in the same row and the
skin around the hole. Replacement fasteners must match the specified material, type and size from
the repair manual, and rivet spacing and edge distance must be maintained.
