A crack (fracture, fissure or linear skin damage) in primary or secondary skin structure is treated as a progressive defect: it does not stop
growing on its own under cyclic loading. Visual detection only flags a candidate indication. The
indication should be confirmed with an approved non-destructive inspection method such as eddy
current, dye penetrant or ultrasonic inspection, and the full length and the crack tips must be
located. Check adjacent fasteners and the underlying structure because cracks often start at
fastener holes, cutouts and skin joints. Allowable crack size and any permitted repair are defined
by the structural repair manual for that aircraft, not by general guidance.
