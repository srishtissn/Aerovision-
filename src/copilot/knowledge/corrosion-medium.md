For medium-severity corrosion, schedule a detailed inspection within 7 days. Clean the area, remove
the corrosion products by an approved method, measure any material loss, then re-apply a corrosion
inhibiting finish. Check surrounding fasteners and faying surfaces for hidden corrosion and look for
the moisture source such as failed sealant or blocked drains.
