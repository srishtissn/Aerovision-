Remaining useful life is a model estimate of how many operating cycles remain before an engine
reaches its failure threshold. It is a planning aid, not a release criterion. A low value means
maintenance should be planned early and condition monitoring should be increased. Engine health from
sensor data is independent of skin damage found by visual inspection, so a healthy engine does not
offset a skin defect and a skin defect does not by itself indicate engine wear.
