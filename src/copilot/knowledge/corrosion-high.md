action: "Remove from service for corrosion removal, depth measurement and engineering assessment"
interval: "within 24 hours"
---
A high-severity corrosion finding covers a large or fast-developing area, or an area on primary
structure. Inspect within 24 hours, strip coatings to expose the full extent, remove the corrosion
products and measure the remaining material thickness against the repair manual limits. If thickness
is below limits the aircraft should not fly until the structure is repaired or the part is replaced.
After removal apply conversion coating and a protective finish.
