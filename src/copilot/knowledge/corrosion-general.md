Corrosion (rust, oxidation, pitting, staining of the metal skin) is a chemical or electrochemical attack that reduces the load-carrying thickness of metal.
Typical forms are surface, pitting, intergranular, exfoliation and galvanic corrosion. Surface
discoloration seen in a photograph does not give depth of material loss, so the corroded area must
be cleaned and measured. Corrosion tends to start where moisture is trapped, such as lap joints,
fastener heads, drain areas and under sealant. All corrosion products must be removed and the area
treated and protected again; if material loss exceeds the limits in the structural repair manual,
the part must be blended, reinforced or replaced.
