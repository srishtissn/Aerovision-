For a medium-severity crack indication, schedule detailed non-destructive inspection within 7 days.
Record the measured length and the location so that later scans can be compared for growth. If the
crack is confirmed, compare its length with the structural repair manual allowable damage limits and
decide between monitoring with a reduced inspection interval and repair. Re-inspect more often if
any growth is measured between scans.
